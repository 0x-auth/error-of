Mac and internet as two fabrics

Each fabric has a smallest cell of space, a smallest tick of time, and a smallest state change. Fix the law in one fabric, carry its constant across, and see whether the other fabric's change stays inside it.

∆x · ∆t = ∆s · c so  c = ∆x · ∆t / ∆s ∆x cell (bits) · ∆t tick (s) · ∆s state change (bits)

Open questions to test

  • Is there a choice of quanta where c_mac and c_net agree, and does that choice mean something physically?
  • Should c carry units of time, as here, or should ∆x be counted in cells so that c becomes cells per tick?
  • A CPU flips many bits per tick and a Turing machine flips one. Is c a parallelism constant?
  • Walk caps (32 symlinks, 20 redirects, TTL 255): do they follow from c, or are they independent choices?
